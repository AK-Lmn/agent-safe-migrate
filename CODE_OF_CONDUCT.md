# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Migration Guardian to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainer, [@Aditya31032006](https://github.com/Aditya31032006), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on how serious it is, the response may be:

1. **A private, written warning**, explaining what happened and why it wasn't acceptable.
2. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
3. **A permanent ban** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards anyone.
